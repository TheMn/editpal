# The editor's words

Knowing these makes it easy to say exactly what you want. One line each.

| Word | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **A-roll** | the main footage: you talking, or your screen recording with your voice |
| **B-roll** | pictures laid over the A-roll while the voice continues: cutaways, screenshots, product shots |
| **Selects** | the good parts picked out of a long recording |
| **A-cut** | the A-roll with mistakes, long pauses, fillers and repeated takes removed. Everything else sits on top of it |
| **Paper edit** | the cut shown as text: the transcript with removed parts struck through |
| **Seam** | the join where two kept pieces meet |
| **Picture lock** | the moment the A-cut is final. Graphics, music and the dub are timed to it, so changes after it cost more |
| **Retake / false start** | a sentence said again / a sentence begun, abandoned and begun again |
| **J-cut / L-cut** | the sound of the next shot starts before its picture / the sound continues after the picture has changed |
| **Punch-in** | a small zoom on the speaker for emphasis |
| **Motion plate** | a short finished animation (3–8 s) dropped over the A-cut: a diagram, a number, a title |
| **Lower third** | a name or label in the bottom part of the frame |
| **Picture-in-picture (PiP)** | you stay visible in a corner while a graphic fills the frame |
| **Chapter card** | a title that opens a new section |
| **Storyboard** | the plan as stills: one picture per beat, before anything is animated |
| **Contact sheet** | many small frames on one page, to judge footage at a glance |
| **Beat** | one moment in the video where something happens on screen |
| **Lanes** | the layers of an edit shown as rows: A-roll, B-roll, graphics, captions, music, effects |
| **Stem** | one ingredient of the sound kept as its own file: the voice, the music |
| **Bed** | the music and effects without any voice. Every language's voice goes over the same bed |
| **Ducking** | the music dips while someone speaks and rises in the gaps |
| **Dub** | another language's voice track for the same picture |
| **Captions / subtitles** | the spoken words as text: burned into the picture (Shorts), or a separate file the viewer can switch on (YouTube) |
| **LUFS** | how loud a video sounds overall. YouTube aims at about −14; speech-led videos are mixed to −16 |
| **Draft / final** | a quick full-length version to check, with timecode on it / the clean full-quality file to upload |
| **Timecode** | the running clock on a draft, so a note can say "at 3:12" |
| **Proxy** | a small copy of big footage that is fast to look through |
| **Hook** | the first seconds that make a viewer stay |
| **Safe area** | the part of the frame that apps do not cover with buttons and text |

# Formats

A format is one kind of video this channel makes: what comes in, how it is cut, what goes on top,
what goes out. `/setup` copies the ones you chose from `_library/` into this folder and adjusts
them; edit them any time. The `video` skill reads the format first; a choice in a video's brief
overrides it, and a line in `channel.md` → "Standing preferences" overrides both.

| Preset | For |
| --- | --- |
| `talking-head` | you, to camera; maybe a screen recording or B-roll |
| `screencast` | a screen recording with your voice; with or without a face camera |
| `faceless` | a script becomes voice, graphics and music; nothing is recorded |
| `podcast` | two or more people, or two or more cameras, recorded together |
| `montage` | B-roll and music lead; little or no talking |
| `short` | made vertical from the start, and the specs every Short and Reel follows |

A new format: copy the closest preset, rename it, change the values.

# Six habits that make the cut cleaner

None are required. Each one removes a class of mistakes from the edit.

1. **Three seconds of silence first.** Sit still and say nothing before you start. That silence
   is used to clean the sound of the whole recording.
2. **After a flub, pause, then say the whole sentence again.** Not from the middle. A full
   restart is found and removed reliably; a patched half-sentence is not.
3. **Pick a cue word** (for example "again") and say it after a flub. Tell `/setup` the word:
   everything from the start of the flubbed sentence to the cue word is dropped.
4. **Leave a breath between sentences.** Cuts are made in the quiet between words. If you run
   sentences together there is nowhere clean to cut.
5. **Record the original, at full quality.** Do not re-export or compress before uploading to
   Drive. A separate microphone track is welcome: put both files in the folder.
6. **Say when something on screen is private.** Or just say "blur this": it will be found in
   the transcript and hidden.

For a screen recording: move the mouse to what you are talking about and hold it there a
moment; the zooms follow it.

A short video needs a short script. If you want 60 seconds, record 60–90 seconds from a script,
not ten minutes to be cut down (`/script` writes the tight version from what you said).
